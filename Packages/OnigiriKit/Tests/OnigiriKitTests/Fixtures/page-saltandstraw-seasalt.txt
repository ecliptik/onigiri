Sea Salt with Caramel Ribbons
Our reimagination of the classic. We spike our cream with just enough Guatemalan fleur de sel to bring out its nuances, then drizzle in ribbons of our hand-burned caramel.
Made with Bitterman Salt

NOTES FROM THE KITCHEN
Call it our gateway ice cream. This is our first flavor ever crafted, first served from Kim's car on Earth Day 2011 to Alberta Street Cleanup volunteers. It's been a staple on our Classics Menu since we opened our first shop that same year. In our kitchen, we believe Bitter+Sweet+Salt is the holy trinity of flavors, and this ice cream perfectly marries these elements. Unlike a typical salted caramel, our goal was to create a salted ice cream, employing the challenging ribboning technique to keep caramel suspended by cooking it longer to achieve the right consistency. Developed with James Beard Award winner Mark Bitterman of Portland's The Meadow, this sea salt ice cream features The Meadow's Fleur de Sel and hand-burned caramel for a balanced, sweet, and slightly bitter flavor.
Partner Story
We created this ice cream with Mark Bitterman, the 'selmelier' behind The Meadow, a family of world-renowned salt shops in Portland and New York City. He literally wrote the book on the subject—the James Beard Award-winning cookbook Salted—and has aided many of our saltier ice cream experiments since 2011.
